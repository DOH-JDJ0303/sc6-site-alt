---
title: Was this introduced once, or several times?
order: 3
summary: Tell apart a single introduction followed by local spread from repeated introductions from elsewhere.
aliases: [introductions, importation, imported cases, travel, seeding, separate sources, multiple sources, spillover, local transmission, community spread]
routes:
  - organisms: Viruses
    protocols: [viral-phylogenomic-surveillance]
    note: >-
      Include background genomes from outside your area, for example from public
      databases. Local cases that form one clade suggest a single introduction.
      Local cases interleaved with outside genomes suggest several.
  - organisms: Bacteria
    protocols: [bacterial-phylogenomic-surveillance]
    note: >-
      Include background isolates of the same lineage from other places. Several
      distinct clusters of local isolates, each closer to outside isolates than
      to each other, suggest repeated introductions.
  - organisms: Fungi
    protocols: [fungal-phylogenomic-surveillance]
    note: >-
      Include background isolates from other places and, where relevant, other
      clades of the species. Local isolates from different clades indicate
      separate introductions.
---
This question can only be answered with context. A tree built from local samples alone cannot show whether they came in once or several times, so the protocol must include genomes from outside the investigation.
