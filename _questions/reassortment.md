---
title: Have segments of this virus reassorted?
order: 5
summary: Detect whether a segmented virus, such as influenza, has exchanged genome segments with another strain.
aliases: [reassortment, reassortant, segmented virus, influenza, segment exchange, genetic shift, incongruent trees, mixed segments]
routes:
  - organisms: Viruses
    protocols: [viral-phylogenomic-surveillance]
    note: >-
      Build one tree per segment and compare where each sample falls. A sample
      that groups with different strains in different segment trees is a likely
      reassortant.
---
Reassortment can only occur in viruses with segmented genomes, and it is detected by comparing the histories of individual segments.
