---
title: Is drug resistance spreading between cases?
order: 4
summary: Find out whether resistant infections share a resistant strain, or acquired resistance separately.
aliases: [antimicrobial resistance, AMR, antibiotic resistance, antifungal resistance, resistance genes, resistance mutations, carbapenemase, plasmid, clonal spread, resistant strain]
routes:
  - organisms: Bacteria
    protocols:
      - planned: AMR gene detection and tracking
        role: Identify which isolates carry resistance genes or mutations.
      - protocol: bacterial-phylogenomic-surveillance
        role: Determine whether the resistant isolates are closely related.
    note: >-
      If resistant isolates cluster closely, resistance is likely spreading with
      a single strain. If the same resistance gene appears in unrelated isolates,
      it may be spreading on a mobile element such as a plasmid, which needs
      plasmid analysis beyond these protocols.
  - organisms: Fungi
    protocols:
      - planned: Antifungal resistance mutation detection
        role: Identify which isolates carry resistance mutations.
      - protocol: fungal-phylogenomic-surveillance
        role: Determine whether the resistant isolates are closely related.
    note: >-
      If resistant isolates cluster closely, a resistant strain is likely
      spreading. The same mutation in unrelated isolates suggests resistance
      arose independently, for example under drug pressure.
---
Answering this takes two kinds of analysis: one to find resistance, and one to measure relatedness. Neither answers the question alone.
